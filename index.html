Our Story

Three friends, one shared thread.

How Cobwebs Design came to be, and the foundation it was built upon.

Cobwebs Design was established by three friends who came together in pursuit of a shared business ambition. Each having studied design and applied digital technologies, the founders developed a working proficiency in a range of coding languages, including but not limited to Lua, Python, HTML, PHP, JavaScript, and CSS, over the course of their studies.

Recognising that their combined knowledge was sufficient to design and build websites to a professional standard, the three founders resolved to formalise this capability into a business of their own. It was this genuine passion for coding, paired with a shared enjoyment of website design and creation, that provided the foundation upon which Cobwebs Design was formed.
